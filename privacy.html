Privacy Policy

Lift Limit Clips · Last updated: November 5, 2026

Lift Limit Clips is a personal tool used only by its owner to turn livestreams from the owner's own YouTube channel (Lift Limit) into Shorts. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

The app connects only to the owner's own YouTube channel through Google sign-in. It requests two permissions: youtube.upload, to upload and schedule videos that the owner chooses to publish, and youtube.readonly, to read back which channel is connected and to confirm the privacy setting and status YouTube applied to an uploaded video. It does not read or store anyone else's data.

YouTube API Services

Lift Limit Clips uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to upload and schedule those videos and to confirm the result of each upload. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose.

Lift Limit Clips' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where credentials are stored

Sign-in credentials (tokens) are stored locally on the owner's own PC, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. They are not sent to any server operated by me.

What is collected, shared, or sold

  • The app collects no data from anyone other than the owner.
  • It does not share Google user data with anyone.
  • It does not sell Google user data.
  • It does not allow humans to read Google user data, other than the owner on their own PC.

Data retention and deletion

The app stores only the sign-in tokens needed to upload to the owner's channel, and nothing else from Google. It does not keep copies of YouTube user data. Deleting the app's data folder on the PC removes the stored tokens immediately. Access can also be revoked at any time, which makes the tokens unusable, as described below. Revoked or unused tokens are deleted by the owner on the PC; no data is held anywhere else.

How to revoke access

Access can be removed at any time: go to your Google Account → Security → Third-party access (or "Your connections to third-party apps & services"), or directly at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, select Lift Limit Clips, and choose "Delete all connections" or "Remove access". You can also delete the locally stored credentials by deleting the app's data folder on the PC.
